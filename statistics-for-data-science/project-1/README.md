# Portfolio Project 1: PetalMart Customer Survey Study

Statistics for Data Science · after Module 05 · about 6 hours

## Business use case

PetalMart surveyed 4,800 customers about delivery, pricing and the app. Leadership wants to know which factors differ by region and customer type, and which of those differences are real. Clean the responses, correct for who answered, test the questions that matter with the right tests and effect sizes, control for testing many things at once, and write a 2-page findings report.

## Data source

`petalmart_survey` is **Coral Petal synthetic data**, generated by [`../datasets/generate_petalmart_survey.py`](../datasets/generate_petalmart_survey.py) with seed 2026. No real customer data is used.

```bash
cd statistics-for-data-science
python datasets/generate_petalmart_survey.py --seed 2026
```

Items q01 to q16 are five-point ratings (1 = very poor, 5 = excellent), q17 is overall satisfaction (1 to 5), q18 is likelihood to recommend (0 to 10) and q19 to q22 are categorical. About 2% of answers to each item are missing because people skipped the question. The responses contain problems to find; finding them is part of the job.

## Ground rules

- **Straight-lining.** Remove a response when it gives the same answer to every rated item it answered (q01 to q17) **and** was submitted in under 150 seconds. Keep everything else, and say in your log why you did not use a looser rule.
- **Weighting.** Post-stratify on age band: each response's weight is its age band's population share divided by the band's share of the cleaned responses. Weighted means and their 95% confidence intervals use `DescrStatsW` with the weights rescaled to sum to the number of answers.
- **Planned tests.** α = 0.05, two-sided. Report p-values and an effect size for every test.
- **The family of tests.** Every Likert item (q01 to q16) against each of region, age band, customer type, device and spend band: 80 Kruskal-Wallis tests, controlled with Benjamini-Hochberg at 5%.

## Tasks and deliverables

1. Cleaning and a cleaning log (Module 02).
2. Weighted versus unweighted estimates with confidence intervals for app ease (q10) and overall satisfaction (q17) (Modules 04, 05).
3. Planned tests (Module 05): delivery speed (q01) by region with one-way ANOVA and eta squared; value for money (q06), returning versus new, with Welch's t-test and Cohen's d; preferred payment (q19) by region with a chi-square test and Cramér's V; app ease (q10) by age band with Kruskal-Wallis and epsilon squared.
4. The family of 80 tests: how many are significant before and after Benjamini-Hochberg, and which survive (Module 05).
5. A 2-page findings report for PetalMart's leadership: what differs, by how much, how sure you are, and what the survey cannot tell us (who did not answer, and why).
