# Portfolio Project 2: PetalBank Cross-Sell Engine

Machine Learning for Data Science · after Module 10 · about 6 hours

## Business use case

PetalBank's marketing team wants the next best product for each customer, within the bank's eligibility rules, and a forecast of how many offers will go out each week so the contact centre can plan staff.

## Data source

`petalbank_holdings` is **Coral Petal synthetic data**, generated by [`../datasets/generate_petalbank_holdings.py`](../datasets/generate_petalbank_holdings.py) with seed 2026. No real customer data is used.

```bash
cd machine-learning-for-data-science
python datasets/generate_petalbank_holdings.py --out datasets/data/petalbank_holdings --seed 2026
```

| # | Table | Rows | Columns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `customers.csv` | 120,000 | customer_id, age, income_monthly, credit_score, city_tier, acquisition_channel, join_month, occupation, dependents |
| 2 | `products.csv` | 14 | product_id, name, family, min_age, min_income, min_credit_score, requires |
| 3 | `holdings.csv` | ~363,000 | customer_id, product_id, start_month, end_month |
| 4 | `offers.csv` | ~583,000 | offer_id, sent_date, customer_id, product_id, channel, accepted |

## Ground rules

- **Recommender split by time.** Learn from holdings that started before April 2026. Test on products that customers who joined before April 2026 adopted from April to June 2026.
- **Eligibility.** Recommend only products the customer does not hold and is eligible for: age, income and credit score at or above the product's minimums, and one of the `requires` products held (`P01|P02` means either).
- **Metrics.** Precision@5 and NDCG@5 over test customers.
- **Cold start.** Customers who joined from April 2026 and hold two or more products: you know their profile and their first product only. Predict the rest.
- **Forecast.** Weekly offer counts (`sent_date` is a Monday). Forecast the 13 weeks from 2026-04-06. Report MASE, scaled by the mean absolute week-to-week change in the training weeks.

## Tasks and deliverables

1. Popularity, item-based collaborative filtering and a hybrid recommender (Module 10).
2. Precision@5 and NDCG@5 with eligibility rules applied (Module 10).
3. Cold-start strategy for new customers (Module 10).
4. Weekly offer-volume forecast against a seasonal naive baseline (Module 09).
5. A 2-page campaign recommendation for PetalBank's head of marketing.
