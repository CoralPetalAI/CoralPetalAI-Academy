# Portfolio Project 1: PetalBank Card Fraud Detection

Machine Learning for Data Science · after Module 05 · about 6 hours

## Business use case

PetalBank loses about ₹40 lakh a month to card fraud. A team of 6 analysts can review 360 alerts a day. Build a fraud classifier and choose an alert threshold that minimizes the expected cost of fraud while staying inside the team's review capacity.

## Data source

`petalbank_card_txn` is **Coral Petal synthetic data**, generated by [`../datasets/generate_petalbank_card_txn.py`](../datasets/generate_petalbank_card_txn.py) with seed 2026. No real customer data is used.

```bash
cd machine-learning-for-data-science
python datasets/generate_petalbank_card_txn.py --out datasets/data/petalbank_card_txn --seed 2026
```

| # | Table | Rows | Columns |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `cards.csv` | 40,000 | card_id, customer_id, card_type, credit_limit, open_date, home_city, city_tier |
| 2 | `transactions.csv` | 1.2 million | txn_id, card_id, timestamp, amount, merchant_id, merchant_category, channel, card_present, new_device, km_from_home, km_from_last_txn, secs_since_last_txn, txns_last_24h, amount_to_limit |
| 3 | `fraud_labels.csv` | 1.2 million | txn_id, is_fraud, chargeback_filed, days_to_report |

Transactions run from January to June 2026. Something changes in the last two months; finding it is part of the job.

## Ground rules

- **Split by time.** Train and cross-validate on January to April; test on May and June (`timestamp >= 2026-05-01`).
- **Cost rule.** A fraud you do not flag costs its `amount`. Every alert costs ₹150 of analyst time, fraud or not. Flag when the predicted fraud probability is at or above your threshold.
- **Capacity.** On average, no more than 360 alerts a day in the test period.

## Tasks and deliverables

1. Framing and a time-based split (Module 01).
2. Leak-proof pipeline and a leakage audit of every column (Modules 02, 05).
3. Logistic regression and a tree-based baseline, compared by PR-AUC with time-series cross-validation and on the test months (Modules 04, 05).
4. Cost-based threshold under the capacity constraint (Module 04).
5. Error analysis: which frauds does the model miss, and why (Module 05).
6. A 2-page report for PetalBank's head of fraud risk.
