# Build 12: Interview drill (45 min)

Machine Learning for Data Science · Coral Petal AI Academy

Set a timer. Answer each question in writing in 3 minutes or less, then spend 15 minutes on the design case. Write as you would speak to an interviewer: the idea first, then one PetalBank example. Model answers are released in the Academy after you submit.

## Ten timed questions (3 minutes each)

1. Explain the bias-variance trade-off and how you choose model complexity in practice.
2. Your fraud model has 99.2% accuracy. Why is that not good news, and what would you report instead?
3. How would you split PetalBank loan applications for validation, and why not at random?
4. What is data leakage? Give two ways it gets into a pipeline and how you prevent each.
5. Random forest or gradient boosting for default risk: how do they differ, and when would you pick each?
6. How do you choose a classification threshold when a missed default costs ten times a rejected good applicant?
7. Lasso and ridge: what does each penalty do to the coefficients, and when would you use each?
8. k-means gave you five segments. How do you decide whether they are real and useful?
9. Why can a global model across 300 branches forecast better than 300 separate models?
10. A model that worked in testing is losing money three months after launch. Walk through how you investigate.

## Design case (15 minutes)

PetalBank wants to approve personal loans in under a minute through its app. Design the machine learning system: the target and when it is known, the data you would use and what you would leave out, validation, the model and threshold, how a decision is explained to the applicant, fairness checks, serving, and what you would monitor after launch. Draw one diagram.

## Self-review

After writing, check each answer for: a definition, a reason, a PetalBank example, and a number where one helps. Mark the two weakest answers and rewrite them.
